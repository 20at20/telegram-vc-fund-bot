# Movable Voice

**One-line:** Voice AI that calls frontline employees to capture the real reasons behind operational issues.

## Overview

Movable Voice builds AI voice agents that proactively reach out to frontline workers (warehouse staff, service workers, field teams) to gather qualitative operational data — the "dark data" trapped in conversations that never makes it into enterprise systems. Rather than surveys or manual reporting, Movable Voice conducts AI phone calls that surface real operational issues at scale.

The insight: most enterprise data infrastructure captures structured data (clicks, transactions, inventory) but misses the unstructured conversational intelligence that frontline employees hold. Movable Voice unlocks this through voice AI.

## Founders

- **Yan Zhang** — CEO. Ex-COO at PolyAI (one of the leading enterprise voice AI companies). Repeat founder. Strong operational AI credibility.
- **Marko Stapfner** — Co-founder. Engineering and logistics software background — direct domain fit for the frontline worker use case.

## Investment Thesis (from Roosh's perspective)

- Experienced team: Yan Zhang's PolyAI experience brings direct enterprise voice AI know-how
- Large underserved market: hundreds of millions of frontline workers globally, almost no tech coverage
- "Dark data" unlock — converts a previously inaccessible data type (frontline conversations) into structured operational intelligence
- B2B enterprise: durable, recurring revenue model
