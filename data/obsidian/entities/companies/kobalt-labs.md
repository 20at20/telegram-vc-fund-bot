# Kobalt Labs

**One-line:** AI software automating compliance, risk, and due diligence for banks and fintechs.

## Overview

Kobalt Labs builds AI tooling for financial compliance workflows — the manual, high-cost processes around compliance checks, risk assessment, and due diligence that banks and fintechs are required to run. Clients include Chime and American National Bank, validating both neobank and traditional bank adoption.

Strong Roosh thesis fit: AI automating highly regulated, high-volume, low-automation workflows in financial services. These workflows are expensive to staff (compliance teams), error-prone when manual, and face increasing regulatory pressure.

## Traction

- **Clients:** Chime (neobank), American National Bank (traditional bank) (as of Mar 2025)
- **Roosh unrealized return:** 4.1x (as of end-2025)

## Investment Thesis

Financial compliance is one of the highest-cost, most automatable workflows in enterprise. Regulatory requirements create a mandatory market — companies must do compliance; the question is how. AI reduces cost dramatically while improving consistency. Large and growing TAM as financial regulation expands.
