# ElevenLabs

**One-line:** AI voice generation platform with thousands of voices in 70+ languages, serving enterprise clients at scale.

## Overview

ElevenLabs is one of the breakout AI companies of the current wave, building a voice synthesis platform that has rapidly become the standard for AI-generated speech. In just 3 years, the company developed thousands of distinct voices across 70+ languages. Major enterprise clients include Deutsche Telecom and TVS.

The company is the single largest contributor to Roosh Ventures Fund I's performance — a canonical example of the fund's thesis (AI transforming a traditional industry — media, communication, telecoms).

## Traction

- **Clients:** Deutsche Telecom, TVS (as of the fundraising deck, ~Mar 2025)
- **Roosh unrealized return:** 836.4x (as of end-2025)
- **Roosh partial exit:** Sold 70% of position at $1B valuation → 83.6x realized return
- 1,000s of voices, 70+ languages, developed in ~3 years (as of Mar 2025)
- Partial exit via secondary market — Roosh retains ~30% of original position

## Investment Thesis

Classic application-AI-for-traditional-business: voice generation replacing human voice recording across media, telecom, entertainment. Massive TAM across any content-dependent industry. Moat in voice quality, language breadth, and enterprise integrations.

## Risks & Open Questions

- Remaining position (30%) concentration risk in a single name
- Competitive landscape increasingly crowded (OpenAI TTS, Google, ElevenLabs alternatives)
