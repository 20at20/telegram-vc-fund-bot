# Alter

**One-line:** AI-powered creator economy tool — Roosh Ventures' first exit, acquired by Google for $100M.

## Overview

Alter was an AI-powered tool for the creator economy (likely avatar/character generation or similar). It was Roosh Ventures Fund I's first exit — sold to Google for $100M, generating a 7.1x return for the fund. The acquisition validates Roosh's thesis that AI-native products in consumer/creator space can achieve quick, high-value exits.

Post-acquisition signal worth noting: the founder, **Robin Raszka**, subsequently launched a new venture called **1337** — and rejected Sequoia's offer to back his new company, choosing Roosh Ventures and Credo Ventures as initial backers. This is a strong signal of founder trust and relationship quality.
