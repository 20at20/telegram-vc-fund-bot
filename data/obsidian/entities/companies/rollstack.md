# Rollstack

**One-line:** AI software that automatically creates and updates presentations and documents from live data.

## Overview

Rollstack automates the creation and updating of slides and documents by pulling from live data sources — eliminating manual reporting work for finance, analytics, and operations teams. Clients include SoFi and Monday.com. The product sits at the intersection of AI automation and enterprise productivity.

Co-investors include Y Combinator, UpHonest Capital, Kima Ventures, Moore Capital, Goodwater, and Notion — a strong signal of both quality and strategic fit (Notion as a co-investor in a Notion-adjacent product is notable).

Roosh Ventures' [[andrii-tymovskyi|Andrii Tymovskyi]] is listed on Rollstack's website as their investor.
