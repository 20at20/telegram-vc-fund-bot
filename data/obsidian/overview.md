## Investment Thesis

Roosh Ventures bets on **Application AI for traditional businesses** — the layer of AI-native startups that sits between foundation models and large enterprises that cannot self-implement AI. The core claim: up to 80% of workflows in industries like finance, healthcare, and enterprise software can be automated, but 95% of GenAI pilots at big companies fail (MIT/Forbes). Startups are the implementation bridge.

The fund focuses on **Europe** as its primary market, arguing that European AI companies (ElevenLabs, DeepMind, Mistral AI) are becoming world-class while remaining underfunded vs. US peers — creating a valuation gap at the early stage.

Differentiation vs. other early-stage funds:
1. **Founders-led**: GPs are ex-AI founders (Reface AI, Zibra AI) with operator pattern-matching
2. **AI-first operations**: The fund itself runs on AI — sourcing, selection, LP relationships (AlphaVC OS)
3. **Skin in the game**: 30% GP commitment vs. 2% norm — extreme alignment signal to LPs
4. **Co-investor network**: 70+ VC partners, Sequoia/a16z/Accel/Tiger co-investment track record
