# Roosh Ventures Fund II — Fundraising Deck

**Source:** Roosh Ventures (internal)
**Date:** March 2025 (based on filename)
**Format:** LP pitch deck, 37 slides

## Summary

- Fund II is a €50M vehicle targeting early-stage Application AI for traditional businesses (finance, healthcare, enterprise software), with 80% Europe / 20% US geographic split
- Stage allocation: 30% Pre-seed, 50% Seed, 20% Follow-ons; target IRR 25%+, target return 4x+
- GPs have committed €15M personal capital (30% of fund vs. ~2% industry standard) — a structural differentiator they market as "15x more skin in the game"
- Fund I ($20M, 59 companies, 2021 vintage) delivered 1.75x TVPI / 20% IRR as of end-2025, top-quartile vs. Carta median of 1.01x; outperforms General Catalyst (1.6x), Khosla (1.52x), Mayfield (1.3x)
- Standout Fund I returns: ElevenLabs 836.4x unrealized (partial exit at 83.6x at $1B valuation), Alter 7.1x exited (sold to Google $100M), Deel 11.7x, OURA 10.6x, Omnea 6.5x, Unit 4.4x, Kobalt Labs 4.1x, Rollstack 3.5x
- Team is founders-led: GPs Serhiy Tokarev and Kyle Ukho built Reface AI and Zibra AI (300M+ downloads, a16z-backed) before investing; 10 years working together, $70M+ deployed
- Fund runs "AlphaVC" — AI OS covering sourcing (Evertrace, Upstream, n8n), selection (Apify, Perplexity, OpenAI), support (Claude Code, Affinity), LP relationships
- Co-investor network: 7 deals with Sequoia, 7 with a16z, 4 with Accel, 4 with Tiger Global, 3 with GFC
- 70+ VC partners in deal-sharing network; 1,900 deals shared in 2025 (+30% YoY)
- Fund II's first two investments: Tower.dev (AI data pipeline infrastructure) and Movable Voice (voice AI for frontline workers)
- Physical presence in London, Paris, Lisbon, Cyprus; 300+ companies in Fund II pipeline
