# Roosh Ventures Investment Thesis

## Summary

Roosh Ventures' investment thesis as articulated in the Fund II fundraising deck (Mar 2025). This page captures how the fund thinks about what to invest in, how to source it, and why they believe they have an edge.

---

## What They Invest In

**Sector:** Application AI for Traditional Businesses — AI-native companies automating workflows in finance, healthcare, enterprise software. Target: sectors where 80%+ of workflows can be automated but haven't been yet.

**Stage:**
- Pre-seed: 30% of capital
- Seed: 50% of capital
- Follow-ons: 20% of capital

**Geography:** Europe 80%, US 20%

**Founder profile:** Do-ers. Execute fast, iterate fast. Deep market insight. Ambition to build global companies. At earliest stages: the team matters more than the product.

**Deal criteria:**
| Stage | Primary filter | Secondary filter |
|-------|---------------|------------------|
| Pre-seed/Seed | Founder quality + market opportunity | Early PMF signals |
| Series A+ | PMF proof ($1M+ ARR, high retention) | Unit economics (>3x LTV/CAC), differentiation |

**Market filter:** TAM must be >$1B.

---

## Why They Win (Edge Claims)

1. **Founders-led:** GPs are operators who built AI companies (Reface AI 300M+ downloads, Zibra AI). They identify winning patterns from experience, not spreadsheets.

2. **AI-first operations (AlphaVC OS):** The fund runs on AI across all four functions:
   - Sourcing: Evertrace signals, Upstream outreach, n8n automation
   - Selection: Apify, Perplexity, OpenAI, n8n
   - Portfolio support: Claude Code, Affinity, OpenAI
   - LP relationships: Claude Code, Affinity

3. **VC network sourcing:** 70+ cooperating VC partners, 1,900 deals shared in 2025 (+30% YoY). Also LPs in 5 funds (Kearny Jackson, BBQ Capital, Space Cadet, Night Capital, Shapers Fund) for inbound deal flow.

4. **Extreme alignment:** 30% GP commitment vs. industry 2% — GPs lose proportionally if the fund fails.

5. **Platform value-add:** Portfolio support includes AI development (Neurons Lab), mobile scaling (Reface), gaming B2B (Zibra), PR, legal, HR, VC introductions.

## Selection Funnel

```
9,400 prospects sourced/year
    ↓ call + pre-screening
730 prospects
    ↓ scorecard + Q&A
483 prospects
    ↓ summary + reference check
229 prospects
    ↓ final summary
160 prospects
    ↓ investment committee
40 prospects
    ↓ investment
15 investments/year
```

Conversion rate: ~0.16% from sourcing to investment.

---

## Portfolio Construction (Fund I observed)

- 59 companies in 5 years (~12/year)
- Stage mix: pre-seed to growth
- Geographic mix: 62% Europe, 32% US, 5% Other
- Sector mix: 40% AI-driven, 60% broader tech

Fund II narrows focus: more concentrated AI thesis, tighter geographic focus (European-first).
